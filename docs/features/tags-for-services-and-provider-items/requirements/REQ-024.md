## Statement

The Tags tab shall show one chart with the monthly spend of all tags for the last 12 months, so that tags can be compared, without presenting overlapping tag totals as additive.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The Tags tab shows a chart of the last 12 months with one line for every tag that has at least one available monthly total, and a legend that names the tags.
- [ ] A month whose total is unavailable or has no data is a gap in the line, never a zero.
- [ ] The chart says that tag totals may overlap and are not additive, and it does not stack the tags.
- [ ] The list of tags and their targets below the chart keeps the current-month behavior of REQ-019 and REQ-020.
