## Statement

The app shall show, for every tag, a page opened from the Tags tab with the spend of the tag for each of the last 12 months, the part of each tracked service in it, and the monthly amounts of every tagged target.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Choosing a tag on the Tags tab opens the page of that tag, and the user can return to the Tags tab.
- [x] The page shows the tag's amount this month and last month, the sum of the months of the last 12 whose total is available, and how many of those months are unavailable or have no data.
- [x] A bar chart shows the last 12 months from the oldest to the current one, stacked by tracked service; approximate parts are drawn faded and a note under the chart says so (the ≈ marker stays on every figure); a month whose total is unavailable or has no data is marked with a diamond and is never drawn as zero.
- [x] A table lists every month with the amount of each service and the total; "unavailable", "no data" and $0.00 look different from one another.
- [ ] Every tagged target is listed with its type, its parent service and its amount for each of the last 12 months; a month without data for the target shows no amount; the target can be opened and its tags edited as on the Tags tab.
- [x] Each month is calculated from that month's data with the rules of REQ-020, and the tag applies to every month whenever it was assigned (DEC-042).
