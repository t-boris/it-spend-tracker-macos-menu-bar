## Statement

The amount of each tagged target and of each tag shall come from current-month data, shall never be reused from an earlier month, and shall never count the same cost twice; when that cannot be guaranteed the combined total shall be unavailable instead of wrong.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A service's amount uses the existing monthly service spend calculation; keys and breakdown items use their available current-month cost; tagged services count regardless of whether the service is included in the main total.
- [ ] Missing costs stay unavailable and are not added; estimates keep the ≈ marker; tagging never changes underlying spend.
- [ ] Within a tag and a service, a tagged service contributes once and covers all other tagged targets of that service: a $100 service and its $30 key tagged together contribute $100.
- [ ] Without a service assignment, targets are summed only when their costs are provably disjoint (DEC-035); otherwise each target shows its own amount and the tag's combined total is unavailable, naming the unresolved service (DEC-034).
- [ ] Different tags may overlap financially; their totals are not additive.
