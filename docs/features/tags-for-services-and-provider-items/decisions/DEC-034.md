## Context

AC-008 marks the combined total unavailable when overlap cannot be resolved and keeps individual amounts visible. A tag can span several services while only one of them is unresolved (for example a tagged key and a tagged project row of the same service). It was open whether "combined total" means the whole tag or one service's part. Checked: overview.md (Assumptions, AC-008), plan.md I-4, main-feature REQ-013 and DEC-024.

## Alternatives

1. Show the sum of the resolved services, flagged incomplete, with the unresolved service listed separately

## Decision

When any service's part of a tag cannot be resolved, the tag's headline total is unavailable. Every target keeps its own amount, and the unresolved service is named. Targets whose cost is simply missing are different: they stay listed as unavailable and are not added, and the total of the rest is still shown (REQ-013 behavior).

## Reason

The user answered "Unavailable for the whole tag": the literal reading, and the overview's own assumption that an unavailable total is preferable to a misleading sum.

## Consequences

One ambiguous service hides the tag's number until the user removes an overlapping tag. The Tags view must explain which service is unresolved.
