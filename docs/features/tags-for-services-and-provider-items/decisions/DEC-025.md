## Context

AC-001 lets users tag every provider breakdown item the app displays. The overview's analysis covers only the money breakdown rows (BreakdownRecord: account, project, line item). Neon shows its projects as usage-metric rows (UsageMetricRecord) with an approximate cost, a different record type. Checked: overview.md, implementation/plan.md, SRC-001, main-feature REQ-015, DEC-027, DEC-031, DEC-033.

## Alternatives

1. Tag only the money breakdown rows; Neon stays taggable as a whole service and by key

## Decision

Neon projects are taggable items, like the other breakdown rows. A Neon project is identified by its organization plus its project name. Its current-month amount is the approximate (≈) sum of its metrics' approximate costs and is always marked ≈.

## Reason

The user answered "Yes, tag Neon projects" (2026-11-08): it matches AC-001 literally ("every item displayed") and the original request ("any element a service divides into").

## Consequences

A fourth target kind (usage project) is needed besides service, key and breakdown item. Its cost can be unavailable when the plan's rates are unknown.
