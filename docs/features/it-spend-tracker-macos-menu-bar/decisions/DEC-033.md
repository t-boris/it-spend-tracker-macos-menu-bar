## Context

DEC-031 showed Neon usage without dollars. After using it the user said: «он никак мне не переводит это в деньги, даже приблизительно» and «Истории нет никакой» — without money the service has no spend and no history.

## Decision

Usage stays the primary information. Next to it the Neon page shows an approximate cost ("≈ $X") computed with Neon's documented formula: usage in billing units × the rate of the plan reported for that project and period (`period_plan`), minus included allowances. Rates (neon.com/docs/introduction/usage-calculations, 2026-09-30): compute 0.106 USD/CU-hour (Launch, Agent) or 0.222 (Scale, Business, Enterprise); root and child storage 0.35 USD/GB-month; instant restore 0.20; snapshots 0.09; public transfer 0.10 USD/GB above 500 GB per project and month; private transfer 0.01 USD/GB (Scale); extra branches 1.50 USD/branch-month above 9 (Launch) or 24 (Scale) per project; free plan 0. The approximation is shown per metric, per project and per month (a monthly history of usage and ≈ cost on the Neon page). The approximate cost **is** Neon's spend: it is counted in the service total, the overall total, the cross-service history and warnings, and every figure that contains it is marked "≈" (per service, per month and in totals).

User clarifications: «ты не должен заменять это деньгами ... ориентировать на сколько это стоит, примерно» (usage stays visible); first answer "only on the Neon page", then changed: «если у тебя есть уже примерная сумма, вот примерно ее и показывай, со значком «примерно», в «total» и везде ... в «history»».

## Reason

The user wants Neon in money and in history, even approximately.

## Consequences

DEC-031's "no dollar amount" is replaced. A Neon subscription entered as well would double count. Rates are built in and must be updated when Neon changes its prices.
