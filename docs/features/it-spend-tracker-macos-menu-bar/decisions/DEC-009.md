## Context

REQ-009 says 'every few hours', which is untestable, and launch/wake behavior is undefined.

## Alternatives

1. Fixed interval of 4h
2. User-configurable interval

## Decision

Automatic refresh runs every 4 hours while the app is running. A refresh also runs on app launch and after the Mac wakes from sleep if the last successful refresh is older than 4 hours. The interval is not user-configurable in v1.

## Reason

Billing data changes slowly; a fixed value is simple and testable, and launch/wake triggers keep data fresh on a laptop.

## Consequences

REQ-009 acceptance criteria are updated to 4 hours and launch/wake triggers.
