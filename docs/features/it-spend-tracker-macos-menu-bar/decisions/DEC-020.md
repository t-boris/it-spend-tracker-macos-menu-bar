## Context

DEC-008 says usage-billed charges on credit-funded accounts must not be counted again in totals. Research (SRC-002) shows that the OpenAI and Anthropic cost APIs report usage cost, which for prepaid accounts is credit consumption, and that no provider API tells whether an account is prepaid.

## Alternatives

1. Per-service billing mode chosen by the user
2. Always count provider-reported costs as spend

## Decision

Every API-connected service has a billing mode, chosen when it is connected and editable later: **Prepaid credits** or **Pay after use**. The default is Prepaid credits for OpenAI and Anthropic and Pay after use for Vercel and Google Cloud. In Prepaid credits mode, provider-reported costs are shown as credit consumption (informational, including per-key figures) and the service's spend consists of recorded credit purchases (plus any manual subscription charges). In Pay after use mode, provider-reported costs are the service's spend.

## Reason

The user chose an explicit per-service setting; it applies DEC-008 without double counting.

## Consequences

Switching the mode recalculates the service's spend and history from stored data; no refetch is needed.
