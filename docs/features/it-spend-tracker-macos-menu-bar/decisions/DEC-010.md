## Context

DEC-002 left the expiry warning period and spend increase threshold undefined; comparing partial current month to a full previous month is misleading.

## Alternatives

1. Fixed defaults (14 days, +20%)
2. User-configurable thresholds
3. Compare month-to-date against same period of previous month

## Decision

Expiry warning shows when a key expires within 14 days. Spend increase warning shows when month-to-date spend exceeds the previous month's spend over the same number of days by more than 20% and by at least 5 USD. Thresholds are fixed in v1.

## Reason

Fixed defaults keep v1 simple and testable; like-for-like comparison avoids false negatives early in the month; the absolute floor avoids noise on tiny amounts.

## Consequences

REQ-008 criteria are updated; daily-granularity spend data is needed for the comparison, falling back to prorated previous-month total when unavailable.
