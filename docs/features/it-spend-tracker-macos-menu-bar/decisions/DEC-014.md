## Context

REQ-007 lacks start/end dates needed by REQ-004, and yearly period handling and price changes were undefined.

## Alternatives

1. Amortize to a monthly equivalent
2. Record the charge in the billing month only

## Decision

A manual subscription has name, USD amount, billing period (monthly or yearly), start date and optional end date. Each charge is counted in the month it falls due, based on the start date and period, until the end date. Editing the amount creates a new price effective from a chosen date; months before it keep the old amount.

## Reason

Consistent with the cash-basis rule for prepaid credits (F-001) and reflects real outflows; price history keeps past months accurate.

## Consequences

REQ-007 and REQ-004 are updated; yearly subscriptions produce spikes in their renewal month, which may trigger the increase warning.
