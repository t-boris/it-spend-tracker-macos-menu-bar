## Context

DEC-007 adds prepaid credit purchases, balances and token usage, but no requirement implements it and counting both purchases and consumption would double-count totals.

## Alternatives

1. Spend = purchase amount in the month of purchase
2. Spend = consumed credit value per month
3. Both shown, only one counted in totals

## Decision

Credit purchases (date, USD amount, volume, service; manual or API) count as spend in the month of purchase and are the only credit-related amounts included in per-service, total and monthly history figures. Purchased/consumed/remaining balance and token usage per service and per key are shown as informational data, excluded from totals, with 'no data' where the provider does not expose them. A new requirement derived from DEC-007 captures this, and REQ-002 is clarified accordingly.

## Reason

Matches actual cash outflow (what the user decides to keep or cancel), is always available even when consumption APIs are missing, and prevents double counting.

## Consequences

New requirement needed for credits/balances/token usage; REQ-002 statement updated; usage-billed charges on credit-funded accounts must not be added again to totals.
