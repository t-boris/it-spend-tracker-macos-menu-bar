## Context

Services bill in different currencies (APIs mostly USD, some subscriptions in EUR/RUB); totals and monthly history need a consistent unit.

## Alternatives

1. All amounts in USD; manual subscriptions entered in USD
2. Each record in its own currency, totals converted to a chosen base currency via exchange rates
3. Separate totals per currency without conversion

## Decision

v1 uses USD as the single currency. Manually entered subscriptions are entered in USD; no currency conversion is performed.

## Reason

Simplest option; most providers bill in USD; avoids an external exchange-rate dependency, consistent with local-only design.

## Consequences

Non-USD subscriptions must be converted by the user at entry time; totals are approximate for such subscriptions. No exchange-rate source is needed.
