## Finding

'A few hours' cannot be tested. The spec also does not say whether a refresh should run on launch or after the Mac wakes from sleep.

> The app refreshes spending data from API-connected providers automatically in the background on a schedule (every few hours)

## Possible interpretations

- Fixed interval of 4h
- Interval configurable by the user

## Resolution

AI: Фиксированный интервал 4 часа, плюс обновление при запуске и после пробуждения Mac, если последнее успешное обновление старше 4 часов. — see DEC-009.
