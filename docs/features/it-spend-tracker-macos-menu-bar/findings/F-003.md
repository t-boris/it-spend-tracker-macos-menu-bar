## Finding

DEC-002 says the warning period and the increase threshold are still to be defined. The increase rule is also unclear early in the month, when current-month spend is partial and compared with a full previous month.

> A key whose expiry falls within the warning period ... exceeds the previous month's by more than the increase threshold

## Possible interpretations

- Fixed defaults (e.g. 14 days, +20%)
- User-configurable
- Compare month-to-date against the same period of the previous month

## Resolution

AI: Фиксированные значения по умолчанию: ключ истекает в пределах 14 дней; рост расходов >20% при сравнении month-to-date с тем же периодом прошлого месяца, и только если разница ≥ $5. — see DEC-010.
