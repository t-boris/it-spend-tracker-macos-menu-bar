## Finding

DEC-007 is accepted, but no requirement implements prepaid credits, balances or token usage. It also conflicts with REQ-002, where spend is only 'current-month spend in USD'. The spec does not say whether a credit purchase counts as spend in the month it was bought or in the months its credit is used, so totals could double-count.

> v1 records prepaid credit purchases (date, USD amount, volume, service; manual or via API), shows purchased/consumed/remaining balance, and shows token usage per service and per key

## Possible interpretations

- Spend = purchase amount in the month of purchase
- Spend = consumed credit value per month
- Both shown separately, with only one counted in totals

## Resolution

AI: Показываем покупки кредитов и потребление раздельно, но в месячные итоги (REQ-002/REQ-004) входит только сумма покупки в месяце покупки (cash-basis), чтобы не было двойного учёта; баланс и токены — отдельный информационный блок. Нужно вывести из DEC-007 новое требование. — see DEC-008.
