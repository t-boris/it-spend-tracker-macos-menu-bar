## Statement

The app shows spend in USD for each service, whether API-connected or manually entered, and a total in USD across a user-selected set of services. A service's spend is its provider-reported usage cost (API-connected services) plus its subscription charges due in the month (any service, DEC-026) plus recorded credit purchases (manual services only, DEC-023). Token usage, and credit purchases on API-connected services, are informational and never added to spend.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Each service shows its current-month spend in USD
- [x] User can select which services are included in the total; the selection is remembered
- [x] The total equals the sum of the selected services' USD spend
- [x] API-connected services count provider usage cost; their recorded credit purchases and token usage are not added to any spend figure
- [x] Manual services count subscription charges and recorded credit purchases in the month they occur
