## Statement

The app stores and displays how spending changed month by month per service and in total, including manually entered subscriptions.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] History shows per-month spend for each service and the total.
- [ ] Manual subscriptions appear in history for each month they were active, based on their start date and billing period.
