## Statement

The panel visually flags keys that are expiring soon and services whose current-month spend has risen notably compared with the previous month. v1 sends no system notifications and has no budgets.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] A key whose expiry falls within 14 days is marked with a visible indicator on the key and on its service.
- [x] A service is marked when its month-to-date spend exceeds the previous month's spend over the same number of days by more than 20% and by at least 5 USD; without daily data the previous month's total is prorated by day.
- [x] The menu bar icon shows a visible cue when at least one warning is active.
- [x] The app sends no macOS notifications and offers no budget settings.
