## Statement

The panel visually flags keys that are expiring soon and services whose current-month spend has risen notably compared with the previous month. v1 sends no system notifications and has no budgets.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] A key whose expiry falls within the warning period is marked with a visible indicator on the key and on its service.
- [ ] A service whose current-month spend exceeds the previous month's by more than the increase threshold is marked with a visible indicator.
- [ ] The menu bar icon shows a visible cue when at least one warning is active.
- [ ] The app sends no macOS notifications and offers no budget settings.
